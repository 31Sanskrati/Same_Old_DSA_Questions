## 🚀 Concept Overview
Binary tree problems generally fall into two categories of exploration. Mastering these is the key to solving most tree-based interview questions:

* **DFS (Depth First Search):** Preorder, Inorder, and Postorder exploration.
* **BFS (Breadth First Search):** Level-by-level traversal.
