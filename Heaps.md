## 🚀 Concept Overview
Heaps are specialized tree-based data structures that satisfy the **Heap Property**. They are most commonly implemented using **Arrays** for space efficiency.

* **Max-Heap:** Root is the maximum element.
* **Min-Heap:** Root is the minimum element.
* **Key Operations:** `Insert` $O(\log N)$, `Extract Max/Min` $O(\log N)$, `Peek` $O(1)$.
